Thus, we must choose a pair of points on the line in order to calculate the slope. I choose (1.5A, 0.10T) and (4.75A, 0.30T). This gives a slope of:  $slope = \frac{y_2 y_1}{x_2 x_1} = \frac{0.30T 0.10T}{4.75A 1.5A} = \frac{0.20T}{3.25A} = 0.0615 \frac{T}{A} = \mu_0 \frac{N}{L} = \left(4\pi \times 10^{-7} \frac{Tm}{A}\right) \frac{N}{L}$ . This leads to  $\frac{N}{L} = 49000$ . Plugging in the length of the solenoid gives  $\frac{N}{0.02m} = 49000$ . Finally, we can obtain the total number of turns in the solenoid:  $N = (49000)(0.02) = 980 \approx 1000$  turns.
