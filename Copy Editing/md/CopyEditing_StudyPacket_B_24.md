## Notes for judges

- ¶1 - Gov. Greg Abbott is spelled with two t's. This also occurs in ¶4. This is a mid-level error and consistency is critical.
- ¶2 - Use the day of the week, not today or tonight, in news stories. The story is being published today, Friday.
- ¶2 wildfires Use square miles to describe the size of fires. It's better to say the fire burned 13.3 square miles or an area about the 20 times the size of the UT Austin campus.
- ¶3 - Bonus points to the students who know Texas has 254 counties. Is this referring to Austin County or the city of Austin? These sound like cities but are, indeed, counties. Austin is in Travis County.
- ¶5 - 445.8 acres is 0.7 square miles. Is that right?
- ¶6 - Students should somehow indicate that kilometers need to be converted to miles. They do NOT need to do the conversion (although they can).
