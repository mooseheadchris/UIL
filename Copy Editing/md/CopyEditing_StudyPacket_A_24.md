## Notes to judges

No. 1 - 'Five percent' should be '5%.' Two separate errors. Fixing one has nothing to do with the other. Indeed, until a few years ago, it would have been correct to say '5 percent.'

No. 2 - In cases that require mention of the scale, use these forms: 86 degrees Fahrenheit or 86 F. So 110 F would also be acceptable.

The pronoun 'they' does not agree with its antecedent, 'team.' There are several ways students could fix this error.

No. 5 - BLS stands for Bureau of Labor Statistics. Students can just circle it to indicate that it needs to be spelled out.
